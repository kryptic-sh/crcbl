crcbl worker-backend gate

A page with no engine and no canvas. It loads the worker-backend gate artifact, announces itself through __crcbl_web_jobs_host_ready, drains the spawn queue crcbl_jobs::workers fills, and brings a real Worker up on each request — the five-step sequence crates/crcbl-jobs/src/workers.rs specifies.

There is nothing to look at. The verdict is read out of window.jobsResult by web/tools/jobs-e2e.mjs over the DevTools protocol; the list below is the same thing for a human who opened the page by hand. This page is never published — its artifact imports a shared memory, which cannot exist on an origin that sends no COOP/COEP pair.
